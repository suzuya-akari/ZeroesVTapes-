# ZeroesVTapes-
Your music, your device. An installable, offline music player with no login, no subscription and no bullshit. Plays mp3, FLAC, M4A, OGG, WAV, AAC and Opus. Customizable themes, synced (LRC) lyrics, soundtrack albums. Completely free, friendly open-source music player.
